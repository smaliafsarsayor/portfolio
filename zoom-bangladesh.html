About The Client

A decade of hope, education & humanity.

Established in 2016, Zoom Bangladesh (formally the Zoombangla Youth Foundation) is a registered non-profit organization under Bangladesh's Ministry of Social Welfare. It's a youth-driven foundation working to transform the lives of street children and underserved communities across the country.

The foundation runs education, free-food, employment-creation, healthcare, and environmental programs — and in 2026 celebrates its 10th Foundation Day, marking a decade of continuous community service.

The Foundation's Work

Core programs Zoom Bangladesh runs.

Schooling Program

Free education across 10 branches — 6 academic, 4 open-air — for 650+ children.

Free Food Program

Nutritious meals for street children, rickshaw pullers, and the elderly.

Poriborton (Employment Creation)

Housing and income-generating tools helping 350+ families become self-reliant.

Youth & Career Development

Training programs preparing young people for stable, dignified work.

Blood Donation & Medical Camp

Community health drives and free medical support.

Environment & Tree Plantation

60,000+ trees planted nationwide to fight deforestation and climate change.
